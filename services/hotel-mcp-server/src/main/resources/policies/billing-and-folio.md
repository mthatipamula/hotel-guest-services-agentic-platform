# Billing, Folio Adjustments and Disputes (SOP-FIN-06)

## Reviewing a folio
Room charges post nightly with room tax of 17.4%. Incidentals (dining, minibar, parking, spa) post to the folio in real time. Staff should explain each line item and its date when a guest asks.

## Minibar disputes
Minibar charges are posted from sensor data and attendant checks, which can be wrong. A first-time minibar dispute of $50 or less may be removed as a goodwill adjustment with duty manager approval, without investigation. Larger or repeated disputes require a minibar audit by housekeeping before any adjustment.

## Parking
Valet parking is $65 per night with in-and-out privileges. Self-parking is not available at this property.

## Corporate rates
Guests on a corporate rate plan may have charges billed to the company's direct-bill account; only room and tax are covered unless the profile says otherwise.

## Adjustments
All folio adjustments and credits require duty manager approval and are posted with the approver's name. Staff must never promise a refund to the guest's card before approval.
