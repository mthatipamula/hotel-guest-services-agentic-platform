# Room Moves and Upgrades (SOP-RM-01)

## When a room move is appropriate
Offer a room move when the current room has a defect that cannot be fixed within 30 minutes (for example HVAC failure, plumbing leak, persistent noise), when the guest reports a safety concern, or when an accessibility need is not met. Always offer the move before the guest has to ask twice.

## Selecting the replacement room
The replacement room must be in VACANT_CLEAN status with no open maintenance ticket. It must be the same room type or better. Prefer, in order: same floor or higher floor, same view or better view, similar features (bathtub or walk-in shower), and distance from elevators and function rooms for noise complaints. Accessible rooms must only be assigned to guests who need them unless no other room is available tonight.

## Complimentary upgrades during service recovery
For PLATINUM members, a complimentary upgrade to a Junior Suite (JRS) is the preferred recovery when a suite is VACANT_CLEAN and not assigned to an arrival. GOLD members may be upgraded to a Junior Suite at the duty manager's discretion. Executive Suites (EXS) are never given as a complimentary upgrade without General Manager approval.

## Approval and execution
Every room move requires duty manager approval in the operations console before it is executed in the property system. Staff must record the reason. After the move, the vacated room is set to VACANT_DIRTY and housekeeping receives a departure clean task. Offer luggage assistance and new key cards.
