# Engineering and Maintenance Response Standards (SOP-EN-05)

## Priority levels
URGENT: safety risk or room unusable while occupied (no heating or cooling in extreme temperatures, water leak, no power, door lock failure). Engineer on site within 15 minutes.
HIGH: significant comfort issue in an occupied room (HVAC not reaching set temperature, no hot water, TV or Wi-Fi failure for a working guest). Response within 30 minutes.
NORMAL: minor issue (light bulb, loose fixture, noise investigation). Response within 2 hours.
LOW: cosmetic issue in a vacant room. Scheduled maintenance.

## HVAC complaints
If the room temperature is above 80F (27C) or below 62F (17C) with the guest in the room, treat the ticket as URGENT. If the engineer cannot fix the unit within 30 minutes, the front desk should offer a room move.

## Out of order rooms
Rooms with leaks or electrical faults are set to OUT_OF_ORDER and cannot be sold or assigned until engineering resolves the ticket.

## Noise complaints
Log noise complaints as NORMAL tickets for security to investigate within 15 minutes. Repeat complaints from the same guest in one stay are escalated to the duty manager and qualify for service recovery.
