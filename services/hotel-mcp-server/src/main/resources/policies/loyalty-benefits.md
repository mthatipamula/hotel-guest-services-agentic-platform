# Aurora Rewards Loyalty Benefits (POL-LOY-07)

## Tiers
MEMBER (0+ nights), SILVER (10+ nights a year), GOLD (40+ nights a year), PLATINUM (75+ nights a year).

## Benefits
All members: free Wi-Fi, member rates, points on eligible spend.
SILVER: 1:00 pm to 2:00 pm late checkout on request, 10% points bonus.
GOLD: 2:00 pm late checkout on request, room upgrade at check-in when available (excluding suites), daily welcome amenity or 500 points, 30% points bonus.
PLATINUM: 4:00 pm guaranteed late checkout, suite upgrade at check-in when available (Junior Suites; Executive Suites at manager discretion), club lounge access, complimentary breakfast for two, 50% points bonus.

## Recognition
PLATINUM and VIP guests should be greeted by name, and the duty manager informed of any service failure during their stay.
