# Pets, Accessibility and General Guest Policies (POL-GEN-08)

## Pets
Dogs and cats up to 50 lb are welcome, maximum two pets per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $75 per stay applies. Pets are placed on floor 9 when possible. Pets may not be left unattended unless crated. Service animals are always welcome and are never charged a fee.

## Accessibility
Accessible rooms have roll-in showers, lowered fixtures and visual alarms. A guest who booked an accessible room must never be moved to a non-accessible room. Accessible room requests are honoured before any other room preference.

## Cancellation
Flexible (BAR) reservations may be cancelled without penalty until 11:59 pm local time two days before arrival; later cancellations are charged one night. Advance-purchase and prepaid OTA rates are non-refundable.

## Check-in
Standard check-in is 3:00 pm. Guests must present photo ID and a payment card for incidentals. Staff must never read a full card number, CVV or ID number back to a guest or include it in notes.

## Privacy
Never confirm whether a specific person is staying at the hotel to a caller. Room numbers are never said aloud at the front desk.
