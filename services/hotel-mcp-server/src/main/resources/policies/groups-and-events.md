# Group Bookings and Events (POL-GRP-10)

## Definition
A group is 10 or more rooms on the same night under one booking. Groups are quoted by the sales team, not the front desk.

## Group terms
Room blocks require a signed contract and a deposit of one night per room. The attrition allowance is 20%: the group may reduce its block by up to 20% until 30 days before arrival without penalty. Group rates are typically 10-20% below the best available rate, depending on forecast occupancy. Complimentary rooms: one per 40 paid room nights.

## Weddings and social events
Wedding blocks receive a complimentary suite upgrade for the couple when 20 or more rooms are picked up. Function room bookings require a food and beverage minimum.
