# Housekeeping Standards and Turnaround Times (SOP-HK-04)

## Standard turnaround times
Departure clean: 45 minutes. Stayover service: 25 minutes. Turndown service: 10 minutes. Rush clean for an early arrival or room move: 20 minutes with two attendants, requested by the front desk and confirmed by the housekeeping supervisor.

## Room status flow
OCCUPIED rooms become VACANT_DIRTY at checkout. After cleaning, the room is INSPECTED by a supervisor and then set to VACANT_CLEAN. Only VACANT_CLEAN rooms may be assigned to arriving or moving guests.

## Early arrivals
Guests arriving before 3:00 pm check-in are prioritised by loyalty tier (PLATINUM first) and arrival time. If the assigned room is still dirty, request a rush clean or offer another VACANT_CLEAN room of the same type. Offer luggage storage and lounge or lobby refreshments while waiting.

## Allergies and special requests
Feather-free bedding and hypoallergenic amenities must be placed before the guest's arrival when noted in the profile.
