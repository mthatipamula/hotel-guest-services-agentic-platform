# Late Checkout Policy (SOP-FO-03)

## Standard checkout
Standard checkout time is 12:00 noon.

## Late checkout by loyalty tier
MEMBER: up to 1:00 pm on request, subject to availability, no charge.
SILVER and GOLD: up to 2:00 pm on request, subject to availability, no charge.
PLATINUM: up to 4:00 pm guaranteed, no charge.
Requests beyond these times are charged at 50% of the nightly rate until 6:00 pm; after 6:00 pm a full night is charged.

## Availability check
Before granting a late checkout, confirm that the room is not needed for an early arrival that cannot be reassigned, and that housekeeping can still turn the room before 4:00 pm arrivals.

## Approval
Late checkouts beyond the standard time are entered by the front desk after duty manager approval in the operations console.
