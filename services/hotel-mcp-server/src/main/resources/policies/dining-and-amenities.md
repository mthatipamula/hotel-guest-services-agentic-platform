# Dining Outlets and Amenities (POL-FB-09)

## Lakeside Grill
American grill on level 2. Breakfast 6:30-10:30 am, dinner 5:30-10:00 pm. Reservations recommended Thursday to Saturday. PLATINUM breakfast benefit is honoured here.

## Ember Lounge
Lobby bar with small plates, 4:00 pm to midnight. Happy hour 4:00-6:00 pm.

## In-room dining
Available 6:00 am to 11:00 pm. Delivery target is 35 minutes. A $5 delivery fee and 18% service charge apply. Allergy requests are confirmed with the kitchen before the order is fired.

## Club Lounge
Level 14, for PLATINUM members and Executive Suite guests. Breakfast 6:30-10:00 am, evening canapes 5:00-7:00 pm.

## Amenities for celebrations
Anniversary or birthday amenity: sparkling wine or a dessert plate with a handwritten card, complimentary for GOLD and PLATINUM members, $35 for others.
