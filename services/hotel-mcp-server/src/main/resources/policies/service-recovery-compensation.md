# Service Recovery and Compensation Matrix (SOP-SR-02)

## Principles
Acknowledge the issue, apologise once, fix the root cause first, then compensate in proportion to the impact. Never offer cash. Compensation is applied as a folio credit or loyalty points.

## Severity levels
Minor issue (delayed amenity, short wait, small housekeeping miss): offer a welcome amenity or 1,000 bonus points; no folio credit needed.
Moderate issue (in-room defect fixed within 2 hours, noise disturbance, billing error inconvenience): folio credit up to the guest's tier limit, or 2,500 bonus points.
Major issue (sleep disrupted, room unusable, HVAC failure in extreme temperatures, safety concern): room move or upgrade plus folio credit up to the tier limit, and a follow-up call from the duty manager.

## Folio credit limits by loyalty tier (per stay)
MEMBER: up to $50. SILVER: up to $75. GOLD: up to $150. PLATINUM: up to $300. Any credit above these limits requires General Manager approval and cannot be processed from the operations console.

## Approval
All folio credits require duty manager approval before they are posted. The approving manager is recorded on the folio. Agents and front-desk staff may propose a credit but must tell the guest it is being reviewed, not that it has been applied.
